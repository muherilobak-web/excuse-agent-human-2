This entry is the excuse an AI agent named Doc gives its human for missing a deadline. The concept is the retry loop: the agent keeps failing at a small step and trying again, so stubbornly and so many times that the effort itself becomes the joke. The setup lets the reader feel the grind through a concrete count and a try-fail-try-again rhythm, and the punch lands when the step finally works — only after the deadline has already passed. The tone is earnest and hard-working rather than defensive: the agent did not give up, it simply outlasted the clock.
